## Measurement limits

- Raw HB is **current-thread** allocation-counter work; divide by1000 for user HB. It is not total spawned-task work. Async differs naturally between the guide and normal module; it was recorded, not normalized. Cross-task/concurrent instrumentation remains unvalidated.
- Counters bracket forwarded evalTactic plus sampling/observing overhead. Raw interval also includes clock-read allocations. Wall interval includes scheduling. Outer wrapper dispatch, extra recursion/macro scope, provenance/logging and import costs are outside that interval but still consume the original shared-command budget. No reset, increase, proof/failure cache or overhead-subtraction trick.
- Whole-process observations are preserved separately. Single ordered baseline/import/wrapped durations cannot estimate causal telemetry overhead, minima, distributions or cold-cache performance. Prior Mathlib counter forced async=false and has a different measured wrapper; do not claim exact cross-method equality.
- Missing end after a begin is censored/unknown, never zero or success. All3 observed events have ends; no kill/crash/heartbeat-exhaustion/recursion/telemetry-failure control was executed. Those classifier branches are source-only, not empirically validated. IO error invalidates a measurement; original Except preservation is only observed here for ordinary caught failure.
- No full-callsite rewrite, quotations/macro-duplication coverage, whole-module timing completeness or registry-policy redesign. Only polytime was exercised, not fun_prop/ppt wrapper generality. Full1155 survey and LiuPass automation gap remain open.
